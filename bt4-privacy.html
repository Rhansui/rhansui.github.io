  • Crypto prices, live large trades and ratios are read from public providers (Binance Public API, CoinGecko, alternative.me). For other markets, indicative prices (possibly delayed) and charts come from TradingView. Announcements come from the public RSS feeds of official institutions (US Federal Reserve, European Central Bank).
